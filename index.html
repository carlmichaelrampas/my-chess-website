Everything you need, nothing you don't

No accounts to create, no engine to download, no pop-ups. Just a board, your pieces, and the rules of chess.

♛

Real chess rules

Castling, en passant, pawn promotion and pinned pieces are all handled — only legal moves are allowed.

✋

Drag or tap

Play with a mouse, trackpad, pen or touch screen. Drag a piece, or tap it and then tap a highlighted square.

⚔

Clear feedback

Legal move dots, capture rings, last-move highlight and a red glow on a king in check. No guessing.

🔒

Private by default

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your games are never uploaded, and nothing is tracked.

How to play

Two players, one screen. White always moves first.

1

Open the board

Head to the play page. No account is required — sign in only if you want your name in the header.

2

Move a piece

Drag it to a square, or tap it and tap a highlighted square. The dots show where it can legally go.

3

Finish the game

Checkmate, stalemate and draws are detected automatically. Use Undo or New game whenever you like.
